Clean Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Clean Pro ("we", "us", "the app") provides an on-demand laundry service. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Clean Pro mobile app and its backend API.

If you have any questions, contact us at cleanproofficial1@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the service:

DataWhy we collect it
NameTo identify you and personalise your account
Email addressAccount login, one-time passcodes (OTP), and service notifications
PasswordAccount security — stored only as a salted hash (bcrypt), never in plain text
Phone number (if provided)So our agents can contact you about a pickup/delivery
Pickup / delivery addressesTo collect and return your laundry
Profile photo / avatar (if you add one)To personalise your profile; chosen from your device gallery
Orders, service requests & reviewsTo fulfil your orders and improve the service

We do not collect precise background GPS location, contacts, or payment card numbers within the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To send one-time passcodes and essential service emails.
  • To schedule, fulfil, and deliver your laundry orders.
  • To let delivery agents complete assigned requests.
  • To operate, maintain, debug, and improve the app.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. How your data is shared

We share data only with service providers that help us run Clean Pro, and only as needed:

  • MongoDB Atlas — secure database hosting for your account and order data.
  • Render — hosting for our backend API.
  • Google (Gmail SMTP) — to deliver one-time passcode and notification emails.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics (see section 4), configured to avoid sending your personal data.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

4. Crash and error reporting

To keep the app stable we use Sentry to record diagnostic information when an error or crash occurs (for example: error message, screen name, device model, OS version, and app version). We configure Sentry to not attach personal identifying information such as your email to these reports.

5. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal or accounting obligations.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access or update your profile information from within the app.
  • Delete your account and associated personal data by contacting cleanproofficial1@gmail.com.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data (e.g. remove your profile photo) at any time.

We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable period.

7. Data security

  • Passwords are stored only as salted bcrypt hashes.
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens.
  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Access to production data is restricted.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

Clean Pro is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International users

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
